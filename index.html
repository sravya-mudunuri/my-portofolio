About Me

My Story

I'm a fourth-year Computer Science student at BVRIT Hyderabad, graduating in 2027. My journey into software engineering started the way most good things do — I had a problem nobody had solved properly, so I built the solution myself.

That first project turned into five. Each one taught me something the classroom didn't: how to design a system that holds together when you add the fifth feature on top of the fourth, how to keep real-time features stable, and how to debug something at midnight when there is no one else to ask.

I got into AI through building with it — integrating Generative AI APIs into real applications and immediately running into the question: why does this model work in some cases and fail unpredictably in others? That question pulled me toward research. I've reached out to IIIT Hyderabad's CVIT lab and applied to the CERN Technical Studentship — because I go after things that feel impossible and figure out the details later.

Beyond projects, I've completed three virtual internships (Tata, HPE and Prodigy InfoTech), an apprenticeship with DoraDAO, and earned a Smart Coder certification from Smart Interviews.

I'm the kind of engineer who cold-emails startups she admires, applies to research labs in Europe, and builds AI products because she genuinely needs them — not because they look good on a CV.
